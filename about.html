I'm Srinivasan Subramanian, a technical lead based in the Atlanta area. I build enterprise systems and study the security and reliability of machine learning.

Background

At Tata Consultancy Services, working with The Home Depot, my experience spans enterprise integration, cloud modernization, supplier data platforms, and applied AI. I combine hands-on engineering with architecture, delivery planning, and mentoring across teams.

My research focuses on federated learning security, language model safety, and efficient NLP. I am interested in how adversaries exploit learning systems, how failures can be detected, and how practical defenses can improve their trustworthiness.

I hold an MS in Computer Science from Kennesaw State University.
